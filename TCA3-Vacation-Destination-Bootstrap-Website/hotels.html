Hostaria San Pietro

Hostaria San Pietro in Trapani's Gibellina Nuova offers seafood couscous with North African influences. The restaurant features chickpea fritters, pistachios, and spices. Try the busiate pasta, prawns, and pistachio, and try the cannolis and strong espressos.

Crocifisso

Noto's Crocifisso, a Michelin-starred restaurant, offers traditional Sicilian cuisine with creative appetisers and elegant settings. Despite its Baroque heritage, Crocifisso maintains a modern twist on tradition, showcasing the town's commitment to Sicilian produce.

Ristorante Locanda Don Serafino

Locanda Don Serafino, a Sicilian restaurant, is located in Ragusa Ibla, surrounded by the Hyblaean Mountains. This restaurant offers signature dishes and a wine cellar with over 200 drinks.
