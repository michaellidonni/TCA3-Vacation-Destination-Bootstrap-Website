Museums & Art Galleries

Regional Archeological Museum Antonio Salinas

The Antonino Salinas Regional Archeological Museum in Palermo, Italy, houses a vast collection of Punic and Ancient Greek art, as well as items related to Sicily's history.

Go somewhere
Museo Archeologico Regionale Paolo Orsi

The Museo Archeologico Regionale Paolo Orsi in Syracuse, Sicily houses a significant collection of prehistoric, Greek, and Roman artefacts sourced from Sicily's archaeological sites.

Go somewhere
Galleria regionale di Palazzo Bellomo di Siracusa

The Bellomo Palace Regional Gallery in Syracuse, Sicily, houses a collection of Sicilian art from various centuries, including paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts, with the most notable item being an Annunciation by Antonello da Messina.

Go somewhere


Sightseeing

Massimo Opera House

Palermo's 19th-century Massimo Opera House, the largest in Italy, boasts an elegant classical exterior and lavish interiors, showcasing the city's rich cultural heritage.

Go somewhere
Mt. Etna

Mt. Etna, a UNESCO World Heritage Site on Sicily's eastern coast, is one of Europe's tallest and most active volcanoes, influencing Sicilian history and attracting visitors to explore its smoldering craters.

Go somewhere
Noto

Noto, a UNESCO World Heritage Site on Sicily's southeastern coast, is a stunning example of Sicilian baroque architecture, featuring the Corso Vittorio Emanuele III and St. Nicholas Cathedral.

Go somewhere


Things To Do With Kids

EtnaLand

This adventure park at Mount Etna offers kids a variety of activities, including theme rides, aqua parks, wild rafting rivers, wave pools, and rides, suitable for all ages.

Bioparco Di Sicilia

Bioparco Di Sicilia offers a unique zoo experience, focusing on conservation, training, and environmental awareness. Children learn about 57 species and explore a dinosaur park.

Monti Rossi Adventure Park

Monti Rossi Adventure Park offers children a variety of challenging activities, including zip-lining, climbing, and swinging, suitable for all ages and abilities.
