About


Sicily, an Italian island, has a rich history spanning 3,000 years. Its ancient Greeks settled in the region, turning it into Greater Greece. The Roman Empire brought success and corruption, but the island eventually declined. The Arabs, Berbers, and Saracens conquered the island in the 9th century, leading to the development of advanced irrigation systems and citrus fruit farming. The Normans introduced the unique Sicilian architectural style. After the Normans, Sicily was ruled by the French, Spanish, and Austrians, eventually becoming part of the Kingdom of Naples. The island faced revolutions, Italian unification, and a separatist movement, but also faced challenges like poverty and the rise of the Sicilian Mafia. Today, Sicily reflects its past through restored old towns, national parks, and ancient ruins.
